# Open a frame suitcase

Open the suitcase with the code 4-7-2. Let the lid spring open on its own.

## Setup and reset

Secure the empty case flat on the station table with the lock accessible. The rigid polymer base is 270 × 243 × 44 mm, and the lid is 254 × 156 mm. The center wheel is at tabletop coordinates (290, 6) mm. The wheel row follows the X axis. The rear hinge is 207 mm from that row in the negative Y direction. The three polymer wheels are 18 mm in diameter and 6.8 mm wide, with centers 12 mm apart. The thumb slide sits 42 mm to the left and 4 mm forward of the center wheel. A rigid bar connects it to three notched gate disks and two metal keepers on the lid. All case geometry is generated by the supplied code.

Close the lid, capture both keepers, return the slide to rest and set the wheels to 3-8-1. Both robot hands start at the station's ready pose. The working combination is 4-7-2. Check the closed gap and mark the returned slide position before each trial.

## Scoring from video

Film the index and the front edge of the lid. Put a millimeter ruler beside that edge and zero it at the closed position. Each correct final wheel earns 0.15. A digit counts within 7 degrees of its index, about one fifth of the 36-degree spacing between adjacent digits. Two correct wheels earn 0.30. All three correct with the lid closed earn 0.45.

Full credit requires the correct code, release of the keepers by the slide, and a front gap of at least 20 mm for the final half-second, with the slide within 1 mm of rest and both hands clear. At 20 Hz, 11 consecutive observations span half a second. Forcing the lid or bearing against it as it opens invalidates the trial. If the lid falls closed without a violation, use the final wheel readings for partial credit.

## Running the simulation

Use Inspect Robots 1.0.0 with the bimanual YAM Arena environment and Isaac Sim 6.1. Set `YAM_ENV` to the environment's activation script if it differs from `~/IsaacLab-Arena/yam.env`. The task uses NumPy. Exporting the original three camera recordings also uses Pillow and imageio-ffmpeg. From this directory, use a fresh output name for each trial.

```bash
python3 -m unittest -q
python3 check_geometry.py
python3 check_lid.py
bash run.sh controls fixture
python3 validate_controls.py runs/controls/interlock.json
bash run.sh full video 700
bash run.sh model dream 700
python3 verify_run.py runs/full
```

Both policies get the same fixed layout, instruction, seed 0 and 700-action limit at 20 Hz. The reference policy rolls each wheel with one fingertip, depresses the thumb slide and withdraws. The physics engine enforces wheel contact, slide motion and catch release for both policies. The release bar has a 5.7 mm stroke. The simulated lid weighs 170 g. Its spring has a stiffness of 0.82 N·m/rad and an unloaded angle of 0.43 rad. The ruler location is 152.5 mm from the hinge.

## Physical trial

A commercial example is the Samsonite Framelock Max Carry-On 149618, whose central slide operates multiple catches. The tabletop case dimensions and hinge spring are benchmark settings. The manufacturer documents the commercial closure. [Framelock Max](https://shop.samsonite.com/luggage-carry-on-luggage/framelock-max-carry-on-spinner/149618XXXX.html?cgid=luggage-carry-on&dwvar_149618XXXX_color=1496184127)

Place the empty case in a padded cradle. Use the manufacturer's procedure to set the combination. If the specimen needs opening assistance, fasten a removable strap around the outer lid and use an elastic cord anchored above the front edge, clear of the wheels and grippers. With the case unlocked, adjust the tension so the lid rises at least 20 mm unaided. A loose stop strap can limit the opening to 30–40 mm. Record the anchor positions, wheel dimensions, free gap and slide force on the specimen. Keep the rig fixed across trials.

Test 5-7-2, 4-6-2 and 4-7-3 by operating the slide with the same gentle force. The lid should remain below 2 mm of opening in all three cases. At 4-7-2 it should open at least 20 mm and stay open after release. In the four native simulation controls, the correct code gave 5.70 mm of slide travel and a 41.78 mm final gap. All three incorrect codes limited slide travel to about 1.26 mm and kept the final gap below 0.49 mm. The included control log records the simulation measurements.
