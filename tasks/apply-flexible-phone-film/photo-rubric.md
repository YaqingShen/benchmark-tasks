# Physical inspection for the revision under test

Record the installation continuously. Use an overhead camera normal to the glass and a second low-angle view with a diffuse strip light. Place a millimetre scale beside the phone in the plane of the display. For the final photograph, clear both tools from the glass and illuminate from two opposite sides so a reflection is not mistaken for a bubble. The physical trial uses a fresh protector, cleaned dummy glass and a fresh liner. Record before and after pictures at the same magnification. Aim for at least 5 pixels per millimetre on the display.

The 150 × 72 mm phone defines a 140 × 64 mm target centred inside it. Use the four rectangular glass corners to correct perspective. The target corners are 5 mm in from the short ends and 4 mm in from the long sides. Mark the intended application axis at 35, 70 and 105 mm on a removable scale beside the phone. Inspect the first pass at each mark. A checkpoint counts when the blade presses exposed film there and the liner fold is between 0 and 25 mm ahead of the blade. A gap that cannot be measured does not earn that checkpoint.

- 0.20 for a maximum corner error of 3 mm or less on all four protector corners.
- Up to 0.20 for coverage. Start coverage credit at the first visible card press on exposed film against glass. Record its video frame as `first_press_frame`, or null when there was no press. Trace the outline of film lying flat on bare glass. Exclude material still on its backing and visibly lifted flaps. Divide its overlap with the target by target area. Award 0.20 at 98% or greater, with proportional credit below 98%.
- 0.15 when the liner has separated completely and is clear of the display.
- Up to 0.25 for progressive application, one third for each passing video checkpoint.
- 0.20 for finish when the liner is clear, coverage is at least 98%, and the card remains at least 15 mm above the phone for 0.5 s. No bubble may exceed 2 mm in maximum diameter, and bubble outlines together must cover no more than 8 mm². Trace visible pockets at full resolution. A bubble may also be confirmed from the second lighting angle. Exclude glare present on the bare phone before application.

Enter the measured pixel coordinates in the real-trial record and run `bench.py`. List the four glass corners clockwise starting at the near-left corner, followed by the corresponding film corners, the flat applied outline, and one polygon per bubble. Give the three video gaps in millimetres in station order. Use null for a missed checkpoint. Provide `liner_clear` and `card_clear` from the last video frames. The same function calculates features and partial credit for the simulation and the physical trial.

## Recording and repeat scoring

Include the whole phone and the moving liner fold in the overhead video. Place a second ruler upright, beside the phone and in the plane of the card's travel, for the final 15 mm clearance check. At 20 Hz, retain at least ten consecutive clear frames. Use the first loaded pass within 5 mm of each ruler mark. Record the frame number, the centre of the card’s contacting lip along the direction of travel, and the fold coordinate. Measure at the phone centreline. When an edge is skewed, use the midpoint of its two visible ends after correcting for perspective. Align the side camera with the long edge opposite the fixed support so the last fold remains visible. Keep the unrounded gap when applying the 25 mm limit. The gap is fold minus blade along the phone's long axis. The fold must still be visible. Score each mark independently. Use the low-angle view to confirm contact and to exclude visibly lifted patches from the coverage outline.

Use multiple polygons in `flat_outlines_px` when the applied area is disconnected. Their union within the target determines coverage, so overlapping traces count once. For each visible bubble trace its outer edge, including irregular shapes. Use the same coordinate system and calibration for diameter and area. Keep original images and completed annotation files together so a second reviewer can repeat the measurements. When glare or the hand hides an edge, take a second calibrated view before assigning final-finish points. A missed or occluded progressive checkpoint earns zero for that mark.

Use `bench-config.json` on the Mac and the 5090. Copy `bench-trial.template.json` beside your real evidence and fill it in. Identify the two endpoints of a separate 50 mm ruler segment in `ruler_check_px` after calibrating to the glass. The recovered length must be within 0.5 mm. For a visible first-pass checkpoint, enter an object with `frame`, `blade_mm`, `fold_mm`, `card_on_exposed_film` and `fold_visible`. Use null for an unobserved checkpoint. Count consecutive clear frames at the end of the trial and record the minimum height of the card in those frames. Run `python bench.py trial.json` for the photographic score. The video observations are entered by a person inspecting the recordings.

A force check uses three separate fresh samples. For each gauge log use CSV columns `time_s,travel_mm,force_N`. Record at least the 0–110 mm stroke at the measured pull speed. The tool summarizes the middle 30–110 mm of each sample. An empty force file list records no physical force test and does not change the photographic task score. A blank trial template cannot produce a score.

If a registration guide hides a glass corner, extend the two visible straight bezel edges to their intersection. Record which corners were reconstructed. Keep the original image. The independent physical ruler check still applies. For the simulation, the final inspection image is a 1200 × 720 native RTX still. The three original policy cameras alone appear in the submitted-format video.

Use the visible turnaround of the liner as the fold. Follow the turned-back sheet from its loose end toward the covered material. Use the bend closest to the covered portion where it reverses direction along the phone. Record the first loaded frame in the marked window. If the turnaround cannot be distinguished in either recording at that time, record a missed checkpoint. The simulator uses the projected physical liner hinge at the same turnaround. A delayed photo cannot establish a first-pass distance.

For physical evidence, keep both synchronized videos at the declared frame rate. Use four separate media files for the two recordings and two lighting views. Take the final photos without moving the overhead camera or the phone. Annotate the left-lit final image and check the right-lit image for glare. The bench tool decodes the files and rejects impossible frame indices, mismatched dimensions and a checkpoint that precedes the first press. When force CSVs are supplied, fill in the laminate lot, gauge model, actual strip width and pull angle in the template.

If the film is missing or its four corners cannot be located, enter null for `film_corners_px`. Alignment earns zero. Enter an empty list for unverified flat coverage. The remaining observed components can still earn partial credit.
