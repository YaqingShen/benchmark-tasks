# Side-offset hook and clean rest

The left gripper uses the hook to lift the molded ring until the right gripper can grasp it directly. Its open tongue can then slide out. All dimensions below are in millimetres, in the same tool frame as the supplied drawing. Nominal assembled mass is 45 g. This is a proposed build for the simulated tool.

Cut the lower hook from 1.5 mm stainless plate using the closed x–z profile in `steel_profile_mm.dxf`. Its centre plane is y = 0. The upright is 2 mm along x. The 9 mm toe is 1 mm thick and its centre is at (−15, 0, 5). Deburr and polish all contact edges. Join the following pieces of 17-4 PH stainless stock, or machine the same shape integrally. Dimensions are listed in x, y, z order.

| Piece | Centre (mm) | Size (mm) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lower side arm | (−20, 16, 17) | 3 × 34 × 2 |
| Outer upright | (−20, 32, 27) | 3 × 3 × 22 |
| Upper side arm | (−20, 52, 37) | 3 × 42 × 6 |
| Acetal handle | (−25, 65, 36) | 44 × 16 × 18 |

The two side arms keep the upright clear of the receiving fingers. Finish and inspect all junctions after machining, joining and heat treatment. Pocket two acetal handle halves around the upper arm. Use a flush 1.5 mm transverse retention pin at y = 64, z = 37. Fit recessed M3 fasteners through the halves at (x,y) = (−40,65) and (−9,65). If needed, enclose a fitted ballast slug inside the handle and weigh the finished assembly to match the nominal mass. Keep fasteners flush and the handle washable.

Use a washable 140 × 110 × 0.5 mm base. Two 7 × 18 mm posts support the handle 35.5 mm above the worktop. Their centres lie at x = −41 and −9, y = 65 in the tool frame. The working end projects over the base. Before a real trial check the finished dimensions and mass, confirm the polished tongue fits beneath a fresh ring and proof-load the assembled tool away from the robot. Use a clean, dry hook and rest for every trial. Physical fabrication and validation are pending.
