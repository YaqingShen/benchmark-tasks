# Remove a milk carton pull-ring inner seal cleanly

The outer cap starts face up on its clean rest. The inner disk is sealed and its pull ring lies flat. The left gripper raises the ring with an open hook. The right gripper takes the ring directly. The left gripper puts the hook back on its rest and holds the middle of the carton top while the right gripper extracts the seal and places it in the discard tray. The carton stands freely on the worktop throughout.

## Reproduce

Use the YAM embodiment in Isaac Lab Arena and Inspect Robots 1.0.0. Use a separate process and new log directory for each trial so the liquid and intact bond are created anew. The CPU data backend runs alongside GPU PhysX. Set the normal Dream-1 endpoint credentials in the environment when using the model policy.

```sh
export MILK_FULL_CAP=0 MILK_FLUID=1 MILK_IDLE=0
out="$(mktemp -d)"
export TASK_TRACE_PATH="$out/trace.jsonl"
inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena --policy yam_scripted --no-prompt --grader none \
  -E device=cpu -E task="$PWD/task.py" -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 \
  -E cam_width=1280 -E cam_height=720 --instruction 'With the outer cap already off, use the left hand and the clean hook to lift the flat pull ring without tearing the inner seal. Grasp the raised ring directly with the right gripper, withdraw the hook and place it back on its clean rest. Grip the middle of the carton top seam with the left gripper, pull the complete seal out with the right gripper and release it in the discard tray. Keep the carton upright and the pouring mouth clean, leave the cap untouched, and avoid spilling milk.' \
  --scorer task_score --max-steps 1800 --seed 0 --store-frames --log-dir "$out/log"
```

For the second trial use a fresh output directory and replace `--policy yam_scripted` with `--policy dream -P model=dreamlabs/dream-1-beta`. The scorer tests run with `python3 -m unittest discover -s . -p 'test_*.py'`.

## Real-trial reset and scoring

Use a new factory-sealed carton for each real trial. The reset operator removes only the outer cap without touching its inside or the pouring mouth, places it face up on the clean rest, and verifies the inner ring/seal is intact and flat. Clean and dry the tool, grippers, rests and tray. Do not manually reattach an opened inner seal. Run each simulation trial in a fresh process. Set out clean, dry gripper pads and a clean, dry opening hook. Record continuous overhead and side views of the ring, both hands, pouring lip and discard tray. Mark any critical contact obscured in both views as unresolved.

The untouched setup scores 0. Credit requires a hook-assisted lift of at least 30 degrees, both right fingertips holding the ring independently of the hook for at least two control steps while the left hand still supports the raised ring with the hook and the seal is intact, and withdrawal and release of the hook on its clean rest. The right gripper must pull the ring directly while both left fingertips contact opposite exterior sides of the folded top within 12 mm of the seam midpoint. All opening credit requires the inner seal to have torn free from its seat. Award 0.35 when at least 25% of the mouth is unobstructed; 0.70 when the entire seal is outside the mouth and at least 98% is unobstructed. Score 1.0 after the complete seal is inside and supported by the tray (directly), moving no faster than 10 mm/s, both grippers have released the objects, the carton is supported and within 5 degrees of upright, and the untouched cap remains face up on its clean rest, continuously for 1 s. Unbraced opening under load, arm collision, specified contamination, a seal penetrating past the inside end of the spout, or accumulated spill above 1 mL makes the trial score 0 permanently. Missing required native observations prevent credit. In a physical trial, record the left hook lifting the ring, the right fingertips taking it, and the left gripper releasing the hook on its rest. At tear-out, verify that the left fingertips hold the center seam while the right fingertips pull directly on the ring. During tear-out, both left fingertips hold opposite sides of the folded top within 12 mm of its midpoint while both right fingertips contact the ring. Use a millimetre scale or a calibrated image to locate the seam midpoint and both fingertip contacts. Use an image taken along the spout axis to trace the exposed area relative to the full bore. Check that both the disk and ring lie within the tray rim, that both hands release them and that the terminal arrangement persists for one second. A simple angle reference establishes upright position. The simulation uses native contact impulses to detect loading.

Weigh a dry catch tray and absorbent liner before and after the trial on a balance with 0.1 g resolution. Include drips on the rests, grippers, tool and cleanup wipes. Convert the total gain using the measured milk density and cross-check it against the loss in mass of the filled carton. For that check, weigh the carton and the same cap together before opening. At the end, collect exterior drips on the tared wipes, then weigh the carton, original cap and recovered seal together. This includes the packaging mass on both sides of the comparison. Count milk retained by the tray as spilled. Discard the opened milk after testing and clean and dry shared equipment. Review the synchronized views for contact throughout the trial. The pouring lip must stay clear of the worktop and grippers. Once the seal has cleared the mouth, it must also stay clear of the lip. The working end of the hook must stay off the worktop, and the reserved cap must remain untouched on its rest. These observations and the measured spill define clean handling in this benchmark.

## Fidelity

Rigid carton, hinged ring and rigid seal. The bond releases at 24 N outward native contact force over two 240 Hz substeps, based on a related molded fitment. The carton carries 98% of milk inertia; volume-tracking particles carry 2%. This approximates upright opening with small spills. Membrane stretching, ring-post fracture, large-pour momentum and tool yielding are unresolved. The reference carton has no measured pull-force curve. The actual package dimensions and force-displacement curve remain unmeasured. `hardware_config.json` records nominal model values. A related molded closure is described in [US6390342B1, Table A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US6390342B1). A second gable-top design specifies a 5–8 lbf upward pull while the fitment is held, about 22–36 N. [US5735426A, claim 6](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5735426A/en).
