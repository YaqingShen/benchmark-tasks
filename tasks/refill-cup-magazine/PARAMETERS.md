## Sleeve withdrawal in the recorded demonstration

From the first logged seated stack (step 420) to the first logged clearance of the complete wrapper (step 565), the sleeve reference point rose as much as 65.6 mm above its initial height. Across all eight cup reference points, the largest rise above each cup’s starting height was 1.6 mm and the largest horizontal displacement from its own starting position was 3.6 mm. These are sampled maxima from one simulated trial. The log records poses every 0.25 s in this interval, rounded to 0.1 mm. It does not resolve motion between samples or measure withdrawal force.

For a physical comparison, seat eight matching cups in the dry fixture and put a millimetre scale beside the upper rim in the camera plane. Record a continuous side view from before the pull through release. Track the top rim against the scale, note any cup that follows the sleeve, then count the cups after one dry dispensing cycle. Use a fresh sleeve for each repeat and record the cup lot and sleeve dimensions. A force gauge on the sleeve grip would measure the peak pull force separately. Hardware displacement and pull force remain unmeasured for this setup.
