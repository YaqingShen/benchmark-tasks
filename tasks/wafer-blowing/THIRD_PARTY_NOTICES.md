# Assets and dependencies

The task code and twelve generated USD assets in `runtime/assets/` are released under this task's MIT license. They use procedural primitive geometry; no downloaded textures or mesh assets are embedded in those USD files.

The YAM robot and parallel-gripper models are loaded from the installed `yam_arena` environment. The underlying I2RT models are MIT licensed: [I2RT repository and license](https://github.com/i2rt-robotics/i2rt). Robot assets are not redistributed here; retain their upstream license when obtaining or redistributing them.

Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, Isaac Lab Arena, Inspect Robots, the Dream-1 client and other Python dependencies are installed separately under their own licenses. This task's MIT license does not replace those terms. Obtain the supported environment through the [official simulation walkthrough](https://tasks.robocurve.org/walkthrough).
